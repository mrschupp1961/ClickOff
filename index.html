Common questions
Will I lose my Kindle books and digital purchases? ▾
No. Digital purchases — Kindle books, movies, and music — are tied to your Amazon account, not your Prime membership. You keep everything you have bought. You only lose Prime benefits like free two-day shipping, Prime Video, and Prime Music.
Can I get a refund when I cancel? ▾
Yes — if you have not used any Prime benefits in the current billing cycle, Amazon will refund the full amount within 3–5 business days. If you have used benefits (placed a Prime delivery, watched Prime Video), you keep access until the period ends with no partial refund.
Why can't I find the cancel button? ▾
Amazon deliberately buries it. If you do not see "Cancel membership" on your account page, you are likely billed through Apple (go to Settings → Your Name → Subscriptions → Amazon Prime), Google Play (Play Store → Subscriptions), or Roku. Cancel through that platform — not Amazon's website.
What happens to my Prime Video add-on channels? ▾
Add-on channels like Paramount+, Showtime, or MGM+ billed through Prime Video do not auto-cancel when you cancel Prime. You must cancel each one separately from "Your Memberships and Subscriptions" on Amazon before or after cancelling Prime itself.
Amazon re-activated my membership — what do I do? ▾
This happens if you clicked "Pause" instead of cancel, or if a family member on the same account re-enrolled. Check your confirmation email. If you were charged incorrectly, contact Amazon support chat — they typically refund within 24 hours for accidental renewals within 3 days of billing.
